# Why most of the watchlist has no valuation band

**Date:** 2026-08-18 · **Source:** production (v0.12.5) · **Method:** `SURVEY.md`,
generated by `scripts/research/valuation_band_survey.py`

Reproduce:

```bash
uv run python scripts/research/valuation_band_survey.py \
    --base https://<prod-host> \
    --out docs/research/2026-08-18-valuation-band-refusal
```

The trigger was one observation — "NVDA's fundamentals page has no valuation
band" — and the first answer was wrong in an instructive way: the band is not
missing, it is **refused**, and the card says so in a line that sits below three
lines of prose explaining how to read a band that was never drawn.

Widening the check to the whole watchlist changed what the problem is.

## The population

171 watchlist tickers. 26 are funds/ETFs, which file no statements and are
excluded from every ratio below. Of the 145 operating companies:

| | names | |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| band rendered (5/5 levels) | 54 | 37% of operating companies |
| scored, band refused | 17 | 24% of the 71 scored names |
| **no score row at all — no statements ingested** | **74** | **51%** |
| statements ingested but no score | 0 | |

**The dominant failure is not the band. It is that half the watchlist has never
been ingested.** A refusal at least states a reason; these names 404 the
endpoint and render an empty state.

### The 74 are pending, not broken

`fundamental_ingest` draws its ticker list from `fundamental_universe`, and until
today that table held the 2026-08-11 breadth panel — gated on **local lake price
depth**, which has nothing to do with whether a name is ingestable. #347
(`8d8513de`, merged 2026-08-18 09:11, shipped in v0.12.5) widened the seed to
every `watchlist_chain` member and put the ingest on a cron.

That cron is `40 3 2 * *` — 03:40 ET on the **2nd of the month**. Statements are
quarterly, so a monthly job is right, but it means the widening does not execute
until **2026-09-02**, and the seed script (`scripts/seed_fundamental_universe.py`)
is manual and may not have been run against the mini at all.

Both resolve the same way, and neither needs code:

```bash
# on the mini, in the app container
uv run python scripts/seed_fundamental_universe.py
uv run python scripts/backfill/fundamental_ingest_backfill.py --tier ranked
```

Cost is 4 UW calls per ticker against a 120k/day budget — ~300 calls for the 74.

## Three defects, fixed here

1. **`_no_anchor` hardcoded `history_quarters: 0`.** NVDA's card read `0q` beside
   a refusal caused by twenty quarters of data spanning 17x. The count is now the
   window the refusal was taken on, and stays 0 only for the three gates that
   fire before any history is read.
2. **The refusal rendered below the explainer.** `Header` teaches how to read
   five levels and a spot marker, none of which exist on a refusal. On a refusal
   the paragraph is now omitted and the reason leads.
3. **The width message printed its own threshold.** AVGO spans 4.04x against a
   4.0x limit and `:.0f` rendered "spans 4x", a sentence that refutes itself.
   Precision now follows the number. The message also names the window it
   measured (`own 14-quarter`) instead of interpolating `WINDOW_QUARTERS`
   regardless.
4. **The width message asserted a cause it never measured.** See below — "too
   unstable to anchor a price to" is withdrawn.

`ANCHOR_RULES_REV` goes 2 → 3 with them. No threshold moved, but what a refusal
row *says* did, and the identity key is `ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING` — without the
bump a same-day re-run would keep the uncorrected row.

## Is a wide band instability, or a re-rating?

`scripts/research/valuation_band_width_anatomy.py`, 246 routed names on the local
warm store, trace in `WIDTH_ANATOMY.md` / `width_anatomy.json`.

The refusal used to end **"too unstable to anchor a price to"**. That names a
cause the gate never measures. A band spans 17x for either of two opposite
reasons, and only one of them is instability:

- the yield **swings** — no settled valuation, "unstable" is the true word;
- the yield **walks one way and stays there** — the window straddles two regimes,
  and its far end is a price from a regime that has gone. The opposite of
  unstable.

Discriminator: `valuation.yield_drift`, the rank correlation of a name's own
yield against time over the same trailing window the band uses. Yield is the
inverse of a multiple, so **negative rho means the name got more expensive**.

Among the 13 names refused on width, 10 have negative rho — the multiple expanded
through the window — against a 48.9% base rate among the 233 that pass. That is a
one-sided binomial **p = 0.039**, so the lean is real but thin at n=13, and a
Mann-Whitney on the continuous rho gives **p = 0.155**. `[COMPUTED, MED]`

**Shape does not separate the two populations.** Monotone share (|rho| >= 0.7) is
38% among refused names against 36% among those that pass; mean |rho| 0.597 vs
0.529. So "wide bands are re-ratings" is **not** a law about wide bands.

It is, however, a fact about *these* names, one at a time, and that is all the
refusal line needs:

| | rho | reading |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| GE | −0.96 | multiple expanded |
| AVGO | −0.90 | multiple expanded |
| LRCX | −0.85 | multiple expanded |
| MSTR | −0.83 | multiple expanded |
| DIS | +0.81 | fundamental outgrew the price |
| APP | −0.72 | multiple expanded |
| WFC | −0.68 | multiple expanded (lean) |
| NVDA | +0.68 | fundamental outgrew the price (lean) |
| NBIS | −0.67 | multiple expanded (lean) |
| NFLX | +0.66 | fundamental outgrew the price (lean) |
| RIOT | −0.32 | genuinely swings |
| APLD | −0.25 | genuinely swings |
| ACRE | −0.07 | genuinely swings |

AVGO, LRCX, MSTR, APP and NBIS re-rated **up** — the multiple expanded through
the window. NVDA and NFLX moved the other way: their fundamental outgrew their
price, which is why NVDA is refused on `fcf_yield` at 17x with a *rising* yield.
Only RIOT, APLD and ACRE are what "unstable" describes.

So the refusal now reads, for example:

> own 20-quarter valuation range spans 47.4x, wider than the 4x limit: walking
> one way rather than swinging (rho −0.83) — the window covers two valuation
> regimes, not one, because the multiple expanded through it

Two notes on the measurement. `yield_drift` grades at |rho| 0.4 / 0.7 rather than
using one cut, because NBIS at −0.67 and WFC at −0.68 would otherwise print
"swings both ways" over two of the strongest leans in the panel. And its first
version was **wrong**: ties took a stable sort's order instead of the average
rank, which manufactured +0.57 out of a perfectly alternating series — caught by
a unit test before it reached the sentence.

## Two things left open

**The 4x width limit refuses 9 names, and they are the desk's names** — NVDA,
NFLX, PLTR, AVGO, LRCX. The threshold is unchanged and the reason is the
measurement in the next section: shape does not separate wide bands from narrow
ones as a population, so there is nothing here that licenses moving it. What it
does license is describing each refusal honestly, which is now done. Whether a
wide-band anchor actually *predicts* worse than a narrow one is still unmeasured
and still wants a forward-return probe of its own.

**Every one of the 71 scored names carries `as_of: 2026-08-14`.** That is not
evidence the anchors job is stale — `as_of` is the **spot date**, the last bar in
the ticker's price series, not the compute date (corrected in this repo's
CHANGELOG on 2026-08-18 after a health check of the form `max(as_of) >= today`
turned out to be unsatisfiable by construction). A uniform Friday
close therefore points at the **lake**, not at the job: at the time of the survey
(2026-08-18 06:13 ET) Monday 2026-08-17 had closed and its bar should have landed
around midnight ET, and it is absent from the price series every band reads.
Check `max(computed_at)` for liveness and the lake's own last close for
correctness. Separate investigation; nothing in this change touches it.
